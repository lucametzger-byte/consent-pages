What data the app accesses

yt-autopilot requests access to the developer's own YouTube account for two purposes: uploading videos to his own channel (youtube.upload scope) and reading analytics of his own videos (yt-analytics.readonly scope).

How the data is used and stored

Data received from Google APIs is used only to upload videos and to show the owner's own video statistics. The OAuth token is stored on the developer's own computer and as an encrypted secret in his private GitHub Actions settings. Video statistics are stored in a local file.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. The app has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
