yt-autopilot

yt-autopilot is a personal, single-user tool that prepares short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It also reads performance statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos through the YouTube Analytics API, to display them in a private dashboard.

The app is used only by its developer with his own Google account. It is not offered to other users.
